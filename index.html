Stonehill Chess Club

Welcome to the Stonehill College Chess Club! Stonehill College Chess Club is open to all students and staff, regardless of experience level. Whether you're a beginner looking to learn or an experienced player wanting to sharpen your skills, our club provides a welcoming environment to play, learn, and grow through the strategic game of chess. We offer weekly meetings where members can engage in casual and competitive play, explore different strategies, and improve their tactical thinking. Additionally, we host free competitive tournament opportunities open to all Stonehill students and staff, as well as intercollegiate events where members can compete against players from other local schools. Our mission is to promote the art of strategy, foster critical thinking, and create an engaging, inclusive community for chess enthusiasts of all levels. Join us to challenge yourself, meet new people, and enjoy the game of chess!
